How to use Jarvis

1. Ask

Type in the bar at the bottom and press Enter. Or hold the Space bar, talk, and let go. You can also click the big ring on the home screen to talk.

2. Get the answer

Quick things are spoken and shown as a caption. Bigger questions open a board of panels. Drag panels around, or press Dig deeper on one for more.

3. Go back

Home returns to the main screen. History reopens old boards. Esc stops Jarvis mid-answer.
