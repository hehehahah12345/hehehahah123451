How to use Jarvis

1. Ask

Say "Jarvis" and then your request. Or hold the Space bar (in this window) or Right Ctrl (anywhere), talk, and let go. Or type in the bar and press Enter.

2. Get the answer

Quick things are spoken and shown as a caption. Bigger questions open a board of panels. Drag panels by their top line, or press Dig deeper on one for more.

3. Go back

Home returns to the main screen. History reopens old boards. Esc stops Jarvis mid-answer.

Shortcuts

Ctrl+Alt+J shows or hides this window from anywhere. Ctrl+Alt+Space starts talking (press again to send). Closing the window keeps Jarvis in the tray.

All night

Blocks sleep and shows a dim clock. It switches off by itself when you quit Jarvis.
